    Swim plan notes & lingo
    The plan: three phases, counted from the comeback start
    • Weeks 1–2 · Reacclimation (days 1–14): 300 warm-up (swim / kick / swim), 4×50 drill/swim by 25, 100s free at moderate effort, 200 easy. 1,300–1,700 yd, about 30 min.
    • Weeks 3–4 · Build (days 15–28): 400 warm-up with the pull buoy's first 100, 6×50 odds drill / evens build, 3 rounds of [200 steady + 2×50 strong], 200 easy. 1,800 yd.
    • Weeks 5+ · Structured work: the same 400–500 warm-up and 8×25 pre-set, then rotate the main set: Threshold 5×200, Speed 12–16×50 easy / build / fast / easy, IM 4×100 IM easy then 6×100 descend. 1,400–1,900 yd. Home suggests the one you swam longest ago.
    • The phase starts with your first saved swim; change the date in Settings if it was another day. The yardages are the sets added up; the chat's plan rounded them up.
    The rules
    • Ramp: the first 4 swims cap the main set at 6–8×100, even if you feel great. Then 8–10.
    • Shoulders first: no paddles for the first 30 days (a workout with paddles is flagged until then). The pull buoy starts in weeks 3–4.
    • Frequency: 2–3 mornings a week. Three progresses, two maintains; daily will bite in month one. A second swim in a week can be the same workout again.
    • Intervals: rest-based for now. Once your main-set times settle (Trends charts them), switch to fixed send-offs, like 100s on 1:40 or 1:45.
    • Breathing: every 3 on easy and drill work to even out the stroke; every 2 on hard efforts.
    • Scheduling: the day after skating is ideal; avoid the morning after heavy upper-body lifting.
    • The runner: tap Start as you push off and Rep done at the wall. A rest range counts down its long end (:30 for :20–:30); Go now leaves early, +10 s waits longer. When the rest ends it beeps and the next rep's clock starts.

    on 1:40 (send-off)
    Leave every 1:40; a faster swim means more rest.
    kick / pull
    Legs only with a kickboard / arms only with a pull buoy between the thighs.
    catch-up
    One arm waits out front until the other finishes its stroke. Long strokes.
    fingertip drag
    Drag fingertips along the surface on recovery. High elbow.
    6-kick switch
    On your side, 6 kicks, one stroke, switch. Teaches rotation.
    drill/swim by 25
    One length drill, one length swimming what it taught.
    odds drill, evens build
    Reps 1, 3, 5 are drill; reps 2, 4, 6 build to fast by the wall.
    4 easy, 4 build, 4 fast, 4 easy
    Groups: the reps split evenly, so 12 reps go 3 / 3 / 3 / 3.
    descend 1–3, 4–6
    Each rep faster than the last, starting over after 3.
    threshold
    The pace you could hold for all five 200s, and no faster.
